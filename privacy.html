Privacy Policy

Slatescope Uploader — effective October 21, 2026

Slatescope Uploader ("the app") is operated by Slatescope, a creator of short explainer videos about buildings, infrastructure and cities. The app is used only by Slatescope to upload Slatescope's own videos to Slatescope's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Information we collect

The app does not collect any data from users or the public. The only data it handles is the OAuth token granted by the channel owner (Slatescope) when authorizing the app. That token is stored privately and securely and is used solely to upload the channel owner's own videos to their own YouTube channel.

Use of Google user data

The app uses Google user data only to provide its single feature: uploading videos to the authorizing owner's YouTube channel. Slatescope Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer any data to third parties. No data is used for advertising, and no humans read it except as needed for security or to comply with law.

Retention and deletion

The OAuth token is kept only while it is needed to upload videos. The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the stored token is deleted.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
