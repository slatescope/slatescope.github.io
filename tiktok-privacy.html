Privacy Policy

Slatescope Stats — effective October 25, 2026

Slatescope Stats ("the app") is operated by Slatescope. It is used only by Slatescope to read analytics for Slatescope's own TikTok account for an internal analytics dashboard.

Information we access

Through TikTok's API, with the account owner's authorization, the app reads only: Slatescope's own account profile information (user.info.basic), follower, following and like counts (user.info.stats), and the list of Slatescope's own videos with their view counts (video.list). The app does not access data about any other users.

How we use and store it

The data is used solely to display Slatescope's account and video performance on an internal dashboard. It is stored only on Slatescope's own systems, together with the OAuth token needed to access it.

Sharing

We never sell, rent, share or transfer this data to third parties, and do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the dashboard. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings, after which the stored token is deleted.
